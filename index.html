What this is

Choose a version on the left. Every version in this list runs in the browser — either the official jar in a page (a real JVM compiled to WebAssembly) or a client compiled from it.

Mods

Mods are yours: there is no list to pick from. Compiled clients are built with the mods you drop in; versions on the real-JVM route take them at play time.

Drop mod jars here, or click to choose — then build.

New installation

Pick a version, add the mods you want, and let a build machine do the work. Only the mods you choose are built in — nothing is pre-baked.

Drop mod jars here, or click to choose

A bake compiles a client, which takes 30–45 minutes on the build machine. The mods that cannot be baked (anything with native libraries, such as Distant Horizons) are refused before the build starts, with the reason.
